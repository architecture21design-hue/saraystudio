The Studio

SARAY is a private house of design founded by Mohsen Masoudnia and Parisa Sayadifar. A refined expression of quiet luxury, SARAY brings together interior architecture, bespoke furniture, and exceptional craftsmanship to create spaces of enduring character.

More than a design destination, SARAY is a place of warmth, discretion, and considered detail, an invitation to experience design with intention, elegance, and a lasting sense of belonging.
